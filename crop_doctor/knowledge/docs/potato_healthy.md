## What to do now
No disease is visible on this leaf. Keep checking your plants every few days, especially the older lower leaves and
the undersides of leaves, and look more often in cool, wet or very humid weather, when late blight spreads fastest.

## Prevention
Plant certified, healthy seed potatoes. Rotate fields so potato or tomato is not grown in the same place every
year (every third or fourth year is best). Destroy piles of discarded (cull) potatoes and remove volunteer potato
plants, which keep late blight alive between seasons. Avoid overhead watering and keep plants well fed: stressed,
nitrogen-starved plants get early blight more easily. Hill soil well over the tubers.
