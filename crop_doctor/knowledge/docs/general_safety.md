title: Safe use of sprays
---
## Before spraying
Always confirm the diagnosis with a local agriculture officer, Krishi Vigyan Kendra (KVK) or extension expert before buying or spraying a chemical. Use only products registered for your crop and that disease, at the rate printed on the label. More is not better: overdosing harms the crop, the soil and your health, and wastes money.

## While spraying
Wear gloves, a mask, long sleeves and closed shoes. Do not spray in strong wind or just before rain. Do not eat, drink or smoke while spraying. Keep children and animals away.

## After spraying
Respect the waiting period (pre-harvest interval) printed on the label before picking fruit. Wash your hands and clothes. Do not wash sprayers in ponds or streams. Alternate products with different modes of action so the disease or insect does not become resistant.
