title: Tomato mosaic virus
---
## Symptoms
A light and dark green patchwork (mosaic or mottled) pattern on the leaves. Leaves may be wrinkled, twisted, narrow or fern-like, and plants can be stunted. Fruit may ripen unevenly.

## Why it spreads
Caused by tomato mosaic virus (ToMV), closely related to tobacco mosaic virus. It is very easily spread by touch: on hands, tools, clothing and plant debris, and also through seed. Tobacco products can carry the related tobacco mosaic virus. There is no cure for a plant once it is infected.

## What to do now
Within 2-3 days, carefully pull out infected plants without touching healthy ones, and take them out of the field. Wash hands with soap after handling them. Disinfect tools, for example by soaking for at least one minute in a 20% milk-powder solution (shown to inactivate the virus) or another disinfectant your local extension office recommends. Do not smoke or handle tobacco while working with plants.

## Organic and low-cost options
Sanitation is the main control and costs little: remove infected plants, wash hands, dip tools, and keep weeds and debris out of the field. Sprays do not cure viruses.

## Chemical options
There is no chemical cure for tomato mosaic virus. Do not spend money on fungicide or antibiotic sprays for it.

## Prevention next season
Use virus-free seed and transplants, and choose varieties marked as resistant to ToMV/TMV in the seed catalogue. Clean tools, trays and greenhouse surfaces between crops. Remove plant debris and weeds.
