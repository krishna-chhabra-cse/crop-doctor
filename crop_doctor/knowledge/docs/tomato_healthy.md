## What to do now
No disease is visible on this leaf. Keep checking your plants every few days, especially the lower leaves and the undersides of leaves, and after rain or very humid weather.

## Prevention
Water at the base of the plant rather than over the leaves. Stake and space plants so leaves dry quickly. Remove weeds, especially nightshade weeds, which can carry tomato diseases. Rotate crops so tomato, potato and other nightshade crops are not grown in the same field every year. Use healthy seed and seedlings. Destroy crop debris after harvest.
