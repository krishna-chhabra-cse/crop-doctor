# Mohanty 2016

- **A model that scores almost perfectly on clean lab photos gets only about 1 in 3 right on real-world photos.** (w1-01)
  - Claim: A GoogLeNet trained on PlantVillage (54,306 lab images, 38 classes, 14 crops) reaches 99.35% on held-out PlantVillage but only about 31% on small verified sets of internet images taken in other conditions.
  - Source: https://www.frontiersin.org/articles/10.3389/fpls.2016.01419/full
  - For our project: Reuse this as the main motivating number. Report in-domain and field accuracy separately and never treat PlantVillage test accuracy as evidence the model works in the field.
